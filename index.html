Client workHome improvement

A design-build remodeling company

A connected toolkit for a small crew doing kitchens, baths and decks: a public website, an office app, a phone-friendly job tracker, and a video ad and posting system.

ViewClose+

I work with a small design-build company on how it presents its work, manages jobs and reaches customers. A public website, an office app, a phone-friendly job tracker and a video ad system fit together into one toolkit that grows with the owner's needs.

The situation

Jobs, phased payments and expenses lived in a spreadsheet. Leads, receipts and payments arrived by email, estimates were written by hand, and crew hours never made it onto the books, so every job looked more profitable than it was.

What I built

  • A public website with service pages and 57 plain-spoken local remodeling guides
  • An office app on the owner's own computer: inbox review, estimates, payments and job details, with the owner approving every step
  • A phone-friendly job tracker for crew hours, costs and what each job really earns
  • Short video ads in six styles, and a weekly schedule that queues finished posts

Decisions that mattered

  • The owner isn't technical, so the office app went from 14 tabs to 5 groups, with larger text and every button easy to tap
  • The first tracker had no menu on small screens, so a crew member on a phone couldn't get around; it now has a bottom tab bar
  • Testing against a copy of the real data caught an update that would have hidden every job, and it was fixed before it reached the owner

How it's built

  • Customer data stays on the owner's computer
  • The AI reads and drafts; nothing reaches the books or a customer until the owner approves it
  • Estimates go out from the owner's own email, only when the owner presses Send
  • I set the direction, research the trade and review every change, using AI tools to build faster
