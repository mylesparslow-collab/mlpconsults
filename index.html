A lead map built from new building permits

Public permit data from four Southern California cities becomes a lead list for the trades that come after the permit, worked right on the map.

Explore the mapClose+

The business need

The trade that pulls a permit already has the job. Countertop, cabinet, fencing and landscaping companies come next, and they rarely hear about it in time.

The solution

  • New permits from Los Angeles, Anaheim, Newport Beach and Costa Mesa, refreshed from each city's public data
  • Each permit sorted by what follows it: a kitchen remodel means countertops and cabinets, a pool means fencing and landscaping
  • Leads worked on the map: status, notes, follow-up dates and directions
  • A postcard preview for each address, as one possible next step

Where it stands
