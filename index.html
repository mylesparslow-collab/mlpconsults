A design-build crew doing kitchens, baths and decks needed one organized place for inquiries, estimates, payments, job costs and marketing, so nothing lives only in someone’s head.

The solution

  • A public website with service pages and 57 plain-spoken local remodeling guides
  • An office workspace for inbox review, estimates, payments and job details
  • A mobile layout designed for crews in the field, tracking hours, costs and what each job really earns
  • Short video ads in six styles, queued on a weekly posting schedule

How it supports the team

  • Larger text and five clear groups make the office workspace easy for the owner to use every day
  • Customer data stays on the owner’s computer
  • The AI drafts and organizes; nothing reaches the books or a customer until the owner approves it

A lead map built from new building permits

The business need

The trade that pulls a permit already has the job. Countertop, cabinet, fencing and landscaping companies come next, and they rarely hear about it in time.

The solution

  • New permits from Los Angeles, Anaheim, Newport Beach and Costa Mesa, refreshed from each city’s public data
  • Each permit sorted by what follows it: a kitchen remodel means countertops and cabinets, a pool means fencing and landscaping
  • Leads worked on the map: status, notes, follow-up dates and directions
  • A postcard preview for each address, as one possible next step

How it’s used

Open the map, filter to your trade and city, and work the list: mark who you’ve contacted, add a note, set a follow-up date and pull up directions.
