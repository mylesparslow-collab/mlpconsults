How it works.

How a project runs, what stays in your control, and answers to the questions owners ask before we talk.

01 The steps

From first call to handoff.

  1. 1

    Pick the first thing to fix

    We walk through how inquiries, quotes and payments move through your business, and pick the fix that pays off soonest.

  2. 2

    Agree on the scope

    You get a written proposal: what gets built, the price, the timeline and any software costs. Nothing starts until you say yes.

  3. 3

    Build, test and hand over

    I build it, we run it on real examples until it’s right, and I show your team how to use it. Support continues after launch.

Built for your business. Kept in your control.

Clear approvals

You approve the messages and rules before launch. Routine work follows those rules, and decisions that need your judgment come back to you.

Your accounts and data

Your domain, business accounts, and customer data stay yours. You receive the access and documentation needed to manage the work.

Direct support

You work with me directly, from the first conversation through delivery and support. Call or email and I’m the one who answers.

02 Questions

Good to know before we talk.

Where should I start?

Start with the free assessment. I’ll review your public website and online presence, then recommend three practical improvements. We can use a short conversation to identify the best fit for your team and budget.

Can you work with the software I already use?

Yes. The starting point is your current setup. I look for ways to improve and connect what you have, and recommend changes when they make the work simpler.

Do I need to know anything about AI?

You bring your knowledge of the business. I handle the technical setup and explain how the tools fit into the work your team already does.

What does ongoing support include?

Website care starts at $200 per month, with hosting, backups, security updates, and agreed content changes. Support for connected workflows is scoped around the systems involved and the level of service you need.

What happens after I contact you?

I’ll review what you’ve shared and recommend a useful next step. When there’s a project to pursue, you’ll receive a clear scope and price before work starts.
