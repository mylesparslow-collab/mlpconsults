Questions we hear most

Does it hurt?

Most clients describe injections as a quick pinch. We use topical numbing for filler and microneedling, and ice for everything else.

Who does the injecting?

Registered nurses and a nurse practitioner with advanced injector training, under the supervision of a licensed physician who reviews every treatment plan.

What if I don't like my results?

Every injectable visit includes a two-week check. If we under-treated, the touch-up is included.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, through Cherry and CareCredit, with 0% plans on treatments over $500.
