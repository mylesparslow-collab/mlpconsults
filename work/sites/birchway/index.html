How visits work

What we treat

What we see most.

🍼

Newborns and babies

First visit within a few days of leaving the hospital. Camila is also a lactation counselor and answers feeding questions on weekends. She says it's her hobby.

🩺

Well-child visits

Every checkup from two weeks to eighteen years. Camp, school and sports forms get signed in the room, so nothing sits on a desk for three weeks.

Same-day sick visits

Six slots open each morning for coughs, fevers, ears and rashes. Call or text at 7:30.

Vaccines and flu shots

Routine shots, plus flu clinics on Saturday mornings from October. We started doing them in the parking lot in 2020 and never stopped.

⚽

Teens and sports physicals

A few minutes alone with the doctor, and sports physicals. Dr. Desai does about forty in one week every August.

📞

Nurse line after hours

A nurse picks up evenings and weekends and tells you honestly whether it can wait until morning. Usually it can. Sometimes it can't.

Good to know

Things people ask us.

Do you take my insurance?

We take most PPO and HMO plans. If you're not sure about yours, text us the plan name and member ID. Marisol checks them between 8 and 9 each morning and will tell you before the visit.

Can we get a same-day sick visit?

Yes, up to six a day. Call or text at 7:30. After hours, the nurse line can tell you whether it can wait until morning.

How do the new-patient forms work?

A link comes by text when you book. Most families take about five minutes. If it's not finished the day before, we send one reminder.

What if we need to cancel or run late?

Reply to the reminder text. If we miss each other, we text you new times, and we keep a short waitlist for earlier openings.

Do you offer flu shots?

Every fall and winter. Saturday clinics run 9 to noon from October. Reply YES to the flu text and we'll hold a time for the whole family.

Expecting? Come say hi first.

Stop by for fifteen minutes with one of the doctors. No exam, no forms. Bring your questions and a coffee.
