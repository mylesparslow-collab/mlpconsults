About the whale

There is a whale-shaped doorstop by the register.1 In 2016 Ines's daughter Maya, who was four, pointed at it and called it the paper whale. The shop was going to be named something much more serious.

Theo buys fiction and nonfiction and knits during slow Tuesdays. Mina runs the children's corner and reads aloud on Saturdays. Gus is the cat.2

If we don't have a book, we'll order it. It usually takes three or four days, and we text you when it's on the hold shelf.

1The return slot in the front door was the video store's. It is our book drop now.2He sleeps on the cookbook table and is not for sale.

Looking for something specific?

Tell us the title, or tell us the last book you loved and we'll guess. Theo and Mina read these, and one of them usually answers within the hour.
