      Questions

      Common questions

      Do you see patients without insurance?

      Yes, and about a third of our patients don't have it. Every service has a posted price, and the Bellwood Plan covers preventive care for a flat yearly fee.

      What counts as an emergency?

      Pain that keeps you up, a broken or knocked-out tooth, swelling, or a lost crown. Call or text and we'll see you the same day, Saturdays included.

      Can I bring my kids to my appointment?

      Of course. Book back-to-back family visits and we'll seat everyone at once, so it's one trip instead of three.

      Do you offer sedation?

      Nitrous oxide is available for any visit, and oral sedation for longer procedures. Ask at booking and we'll plan for it.

      How do payment plans work?

      Treatment over $500 can be split into 6 or 12 monthly payments at 0% through CareCredit or Sunbit. Approval takes about a minute at the front desk.
