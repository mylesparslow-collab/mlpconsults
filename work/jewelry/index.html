Questions

Do I need Shopify?

No. We can run checkout through Stripe on its own, or plug the site into a Shopify store you already have so inventory and orders stay where they are.

Can you use my own photos?

Yes, and for pieces you already have, real photos are best. Renders are for settings you make to order, metals you haven't shot, and the 3D designer.

What does the ring designer need from me?

The heads, bands and accents you offer, your metal prices and your stone price rules. We model your settings to scale so what customers build is what you can make.

Who owns the site?

You do: the domain, the code and the content. The care plan is optional and you can leave it any month.

Where do bookings and orders go?

To the owner dashboard and your email. Orders also land in Stripe or Shopify, and bookings can sync to your Google Calendar.
